A corrupt practice is offering, giving, receiving or soliciting anything of value, directly or indirectly, in order to improperly influence the actions of another party. In procurement this typically shows up as bribes or kickbacks to officials who can steer specifications, evaluations or awards. Indicators in documents are usually indirect: unexplained agent or commission fees, decisions that consistently favour one firm, or evaluation outcomes that do not follow the stated criteria.
