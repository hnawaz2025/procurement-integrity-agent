Except in emergencies, procurement should follow planned steps. Extremely fast or slow steps can indicate wrongdoing: very short bidding periods that limit competition, long or deliberate delays in evaluation, or repeated extensions of bid guarantees. Defining expected durations for each step allows routine checks.
