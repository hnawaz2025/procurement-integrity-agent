Significant post-award changes can be justified but always require careful review: contract value, quantities, unit costs, or reduced quality for the same price. Variation orders and amendments are too often treated as routine. Be particularly concerned when a single contract attracts multiple unplanned changes or extensions, or a large increase soon after award.
