Every AI-assisted review requires human sign-off. Escalate to priority review when there is a high-severity signal, a network link between competing bidders, evidence of document tampering or embedded instructions, or low confidence in the analysis. Reviewers record accept, reject or needs-information decisions, which feed back into screening thresholds.
