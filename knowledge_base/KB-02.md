A fraudulent practice is any act or omission, including a misrepresentation, that knowingly or recklessly misleads (or attempts to mislead) a party in order to obtain a financial or other benefit or to avoid an obligation. Examples include falsified experience certificates, fake bid securities, misstated qualifications, or invoices for works not delivered.
