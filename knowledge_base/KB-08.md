title: "Overpricing"

practice: corrupt
source: "Warning Signs of Fraud and Corruption in Procurement (World Bank Group, INT brochure)"

Money used for bribes often comes from overpricing contracts through simple frauds. Ensuring value for money reduces integrity risk. Warning signs include bid prices or components unjustifiably above the cost estimate, inflated agent fees, and goods delivered below the specified quality at full price.
