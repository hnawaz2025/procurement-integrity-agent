---
id: KB-10
title: "Bidding patterns: unlikely similarities between bids"
category: red_flag
practice: collusive
source: "Warning Signs of Fraud and Corruption in Procurement (World Bank Group, INT brochure)"
url: "https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/223241573576857116/pdf/Warning-Signs-of-Fraud-and-Corruption-in-Procurement.pdf"

Close review of bid documents can reveal unlikely similarities between competing bids: identical formatting or identical spelling mistakes, the same people in key positions, competing bids that differ by an exact percentage, a large share of identical unit prices across bidders, or unusually large last-minute discounts. Tightly clustered prices are a signal worth reviewing, not proof of collusion.
