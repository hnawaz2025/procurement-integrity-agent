The World Bank Group sanctions firms and individuals only after a formal process: INT investigates and, where evidence supports it, submits a case; an independent first tier (the Office of Suspension and Debarment) and, if contested, the Sanctions Board decide. Respondents can respond to the accusations. Screening outputs are therefore indicators for human follow-up, never determinations of wrongdoing.
