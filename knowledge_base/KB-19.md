---
id: KB-19
title: "Tailored or restrictive specifications"
category: red_flag
practice: collusive
source: "Warning Signs of Fraud and Corruption in Procurement (World Bank Group, INT brochure)"
url: "https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/223241573576857116/pdf/Warning-Signs-of-Fraud-and-Corruption-in-Procurement.pdf"
---
Narrow technical specifications that only one bidder can meet, such as brand names without 'or equivalent', unusual certifications, or experience requirements matching one firm's profile, can indicate bid rigging or collusion, and are a common source of complaints.
