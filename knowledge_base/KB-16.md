---
id: KB-16
title: "Bait and switch"
category: red_flag
practice: fraudulent
source: "Warning Signs of Fraud and Corruption in Procurement (World Bank Group, INT brochure)"
url: "https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/223241573576857116/pdf/Warning-Signs-of-Fraud-and-Corruption-in-Procurement.pdf"
---
A firm may bid under false pretences, promising key staff, equipment or facilities it knows it cannot deliver, then substituting soon after signing. Early substitution suggests the intent was known beforehand. This is a form of fraud.
