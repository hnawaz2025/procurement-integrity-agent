id: KB-13
title: "Viable bids rejected"
category: red_flag
practice: corrupt
source: "Warning Signs of Fraud and Corruption in Procurement (World Bank Group, INT brochure)"
url: "https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/223241573576857116/pdf/Warning-Signs-of-Fraud-and-Corruption-in-Procurement.pdf"

Rejecting bids without good reason can indicate an attempt to rig the process, for example an official pressuring the evaluation committee to alter scores or find excuses for rejection. The lowest bid is not always the best value, but every bid should be evaluated carefully and fairly, and the reasons for passing over a lower compliant bid should be documented.
