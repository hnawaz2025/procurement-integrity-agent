Large packages are sometimes split to let smaller local firms compete. But splitting can also be used to avoid competition from larger firms and to stay below thresholds that trigger closer scrutiny, creating opportunities for fraud, corruption or collusion. Watch for several contracts to the same supplier, from the same entity, in a short period, each just under a review or method threshold.
