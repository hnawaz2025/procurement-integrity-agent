A collusive ring is a group of companies that secretly cooperate to rig bids. The ring may designate a winner and make the process look competitive; the designated winner may prepare the other bids, dictate their prices, or use fake companies, subsidiaries or affiliates to submit bids designed to lose. Collusion can inflate costs well above reasonable estimates and distort whole markets.
