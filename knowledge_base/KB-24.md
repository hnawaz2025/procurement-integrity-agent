Prototype settings (illustrative, configurable, not World Bank policy): bid spread under 2% across three or more bids; contract value within 10% below the review threshold; open bidding windows under 21 days or RFQ windows under 7 days; amendments above 15% of contract value; firms registered under 90 days before the tender. Real thresholds depend on procurement method, country and the applicable procurement plan.
