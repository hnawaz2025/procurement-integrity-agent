id: KB-14
title: "Many awards to one company"
category: red_flag
practice: corrupt
source: "Warning Signs of Fraud and Corruption in Procurement (World Bank Group, INT brochure)"
url: "https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/223241573576857116/pdf/Warning-Signs-of-Fraud-and-Corruption-in-Procurement.pdf"

Something may be seriously wrong when one company wins many contracts without a clear market advantage. It may reflect weak competition or a client preference, but it needs close review, especially with exceptions to the procurement plan. A process repeatedly skewed toward one company, or the same bidder repeatedly losing, can be due to fraud, corruption, collusion or coercion.
