Fraud and corruption often surface as poor quality works or services, for example a contractor who paid bribes recovering costs by under-delivering. Abnormally low bids also need scrutiny, since the contractor may cut quality, safety or environmental standards or seek claims later. Effective contract monitoring from award to completion mitigates these risks.
