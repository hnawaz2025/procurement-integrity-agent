id: KB-05
title: "Obstructive practice (definition)"
category: sanctionable_practice
practice: obstructive
source: "World Bank Anti-Corruption Guidelines (user-friendly version)"
url: "https://www.worldbank.org/content/dam/documents/sanctions/other-documents/osd/User%20Friendly%20Version%20of%20the%20Anti-Corruption%20Guidelines.pdf"
---
An obstructive practice covers deliberately destroying, falsifying, altering or concealing evidence material to an investigation, making false statements to investigators, or threatening or intimidating parties to prevent them from disclosing information, as well as acts intended to impede the World Bank's contractual rights of audit and access to information.
