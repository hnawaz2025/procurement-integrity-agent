A collusive practice is an arrangement between two or more parties designed to achieve an improper purpose, including improperly influencing the actions of another party. In procurement this includes bid rigging: competitors agreeing in advance who will win, submitting deliberately losing (cover) bids, rotating wins, or dividing markets.
