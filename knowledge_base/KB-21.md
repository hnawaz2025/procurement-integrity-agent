Write risk signals, not conclusions. Use phrasing such as 'indicator consistent with', 'warrants review', 'pattern associated with'. Never state that a person or firm committed fraud, is corrupt or is guilty. Name the evidence, the guidance it relates to, and what would confirm or dispel the concern.
