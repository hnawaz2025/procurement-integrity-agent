# Bid Evaluation Report: Supply of Portable Ultrasound Units for District Hospitals

Tender reference: VL-CMS-2026-022
Procuring entity: Ministry of Health - Central Medical Stores
Procurement method: Open competitive bidding
Currency: USD
Estimated contract value: USD 640,000
Review threshold: USD 100,000
Invitation published: 2026-03-03
Submission deadline: 2026-04-07

## Technical specifications (extract)

- Units must be SonaView X500 portable ultrasound systems; no equivalent models will be accepted.
- The supplier must have delivered identical SonaView X500 units to the Ministry of Health within the last 24 months.
- Training must be delivered by a trainer certified by SonaView in Velor City.

Pinnacle Medical Group and Everline Medical LLC offered equivalent units from other manufacturers with comparable or better specifications and were declared non-responsive for not offering the SonaView X500.

Awarded to: Westbrook Health Logistics Ltd
Contract value: USD 712,000
Award rationale: Only substantially responsive bid.
