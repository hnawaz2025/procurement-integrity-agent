A fake or shell company exists in name only: often no registration number, no substantial assets, no permanent facilities or employees. Some investigations have found fake firms set up by project personnel. Simple checks such as corporate registries, websites and directories can reveal them. Recently registered firms, or firms that share addresses, phone numbers, bank accounts or directors with other bidders, deserve closer review.
