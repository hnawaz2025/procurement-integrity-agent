Shared directors, addresses, phone numbers or bank accounts between competing bidders are strong indicators of non-independent bids, but have innocent explanations (shared registered agents, family businesses, business centres). Corroborate with bidding history: co-bidding frequency, rotation of wins, stable cover-bid margins. A path through a recently registered or shell entity raises the concern.
